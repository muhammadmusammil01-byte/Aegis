How It Works: A to Z

1

Ingestion

Creator pastes a social media link. Backend generates a unique verification token (e.g., AEGIS-X9F2).

2

Verification

Creator adds the token to their social bio. Aegis scraper confirms ownership and authenticity.

3

Vaulting

System downloads a master copy to encrypted storage, preventing link rot and preserving value.

4

Licensing

Consumer purchases the rights. System issues a signed Digital Rights Certificate.

5

Passive Income

Consumer resells the license. The ledger automatically wires 10% back to the original creator.
